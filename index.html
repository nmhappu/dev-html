About Laluz Garage

Precision electric vehicle engineering, diagnostics, and repairs

Founded by Aswalal Asokan in Cherthala, Kerala, Laluz Garage is dedicated to solving the most complex electrical, electronic, and software-related challenges faced by modern electric vehicle owners.

Modern electric scooters require far more than standard mechanical spanners — they depend on complex CAN bus networks, microcontrollers, high-voltage battery management systems (BMS), and vehicle control units (VCU). Operating in partnership with Prince Traders in Kodathikavala, Cherthala, Laluz Garage brings high-end diagnostics, component-level board rework, and professional harness rebuilds directly to EV riders.
