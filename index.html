About Laluz Garage

Precision electric vehicle engineering, diagnostics, and repairs

Founded by Aswalal Asokan in Cherthala, Kerala, Laluz Garage is dedicated to solving the most complex electrical, electronic, and software-related challenges faced by modern electric vehicle owners.

Modern electric scooters require far more than standard mechanical spanners — they depend on complex CAN bus networks, microcontrollers, high-voltage battery management systems (BMS), and vehicle control units (VCU). Operating in partnership with Prince Traders in Kodathikavala, Cherthala, Laluz Garage brings high-end diagnostics, component-level board rework, and professional harness rebuilds directly to EV riders.

Case Studies & Field Work

Real-world technical troubleshooting and rebuilds documented by Laluz Garage

Ola S1 Pro (Gen 1) Complete Harness & VCU Recovery

Diagnosed recurring thermal cutoff and bootlooping issues caused by harness insulation friction against the internal frame. Stripped degraded loom, isolated frayed CAN bus lines, repaired damaged traces on the VCU board, and re-routed harness with high-temperature flame-retardant sleeving.

Ola Electric VCU Rework CAN Bus Tracing Harness Rebuild

BMS Cell Balancing & Power-Cut Remediation

Investigated a multi-brand electric scooter experiencing sudden motor cutoff at approximately 30% state of charge. Traced a 280mV imbalance across cell banks triggering the BMS low-voltage threshold prematurely. Performed active balancing and load testing, returning the pack to full range capacity.

Lithium-Ion Pack Active Balancing BMS Calibration Range Recovery

Monsoon Water Ingress & Throttle Sensor Overhaul

Resolved an erratic accelerator lockout on an EV scooter following heavy monsoon rains. Disassembled the handlebar switch cluster and throttle Hall sensor, eliminated corrosion with ultrasonic cleaning, replaced oxidized terminal pins, and applied hydrophobic conformal silicone coating.

Water Ingress Throttle Calibration Silicone Weatherproofing Connector Pin Rework
