Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective: Immediately

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled on this personal portfolio website (about.appu.dev), created and maintained by Appu.

I respect your personal privacy. Because this is an individual developer portfolio rather than a commercial service, data collection is kept to the absolute minimum necessary to host and display the website.

2. Information Collection & Usage

Personal Information: You are not required to provide any personal data, register an account, or complete forms to browse this website. The only personal information received is what you choose to voluntarily submit when reaching out via email.

Hosting Logs: Like most websites, the static hosting provider (e.g., GitHub Pages, Cloudflare, or web server) may automatically log standard request data. This may include:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system and device type
  • Referring web page URLs
  • Date and time of requests
  • Anonymized IP addresses for security and abuse prevention

3. Cookies & Local Storage

No Tracking Cookies: This website does not use tracking cookies, advertising pixels, marketing trackers, or invasive analytics services.

Local Storage: The site utilizes your browser's local storage (localStorage) strictly to save your preferred visual color theme (Light or Dark mode). This preference stays on your local device and is never transmitted or shared.

4. Third-Party Links & Services

This website contains links to third-party platforms, such as GitHub, LinkedIn, and X (Twitter). Clicking on these external links will redirect you to platforms operated by independent parties.

I do not control and cannot be responsible for the privacy practices, content, or policies of third-party websites. Please review the privacy policies of those individual platforms when visiting them.

5. Data Retention & Your Rights

Any communications sent to me (such as direct emails) are kept strictly confidential and retained solely for the purpose of communicating with you.

You have the right to request access to or deletion of any email correspondence you have sent. To request this, simply send an email to the contact address provided below.
