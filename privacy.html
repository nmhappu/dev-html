Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026 • Laluz Garage • Cherthala, Kerala

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how inquiries, communications, and website interactions are managed by Laluz Garage, operated by Aswalal Asokan based in Cherthala, Kerala.

We respect your personal privacy. Our website serves as an informational hub and service portfolio for our electric vehicle diagnostics and technical support. We adhere to a strict minimal-data philosophy.

2. Information Collection & Usage

Voluntary Inquiries: When you contact us via WhatsApp, phone calls, or social media messages regarding EV service, diagnostic appointments, or technical questions, we use your contact details solely to communicate with you about your vehicle.

Server Logs: Our website hosting provider automatically records standard, non-identifying server request logs (such as request timestamps, browser user-agent, and general network region) strictly for performance, uptime monitoring, and security defense.

3. Cookies & Local Storage

No Third-Party Tracking Cookies: We do not deploy third-party advertising cookies, marketing pixels, or invasive behavioral telemetry.

Theme Preference (Local Storage): The site uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) exclusively to remember your preferred visual display mode (Light or Dark theme). This preference remains locally on your device and is never sent to any server.

4. External Services & Third Parties

Our site includes direct links to external services such as Instagram (@laluzgarage) and WhatsApp. Any interaction with these platforms is governed by their respective privacy policies and terms.
