I made a thing!

[LaluZ Garage](https://github.com/nmhappu/laluzgarage), a workshop management app. It tracks vehicle maintenance through job cards and has a built-in parts inventory.

It has two frameworks, React web and Native Android (Jetpack Compose), both running on Firebase.
